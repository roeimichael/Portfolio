## 2. The design "multiplier" — how 10 ideas become 100 listings

This is the engine behind your "cover many markets, swap the target" idea. You take **one
product** and re-skin it in different **aesthetics** — each becomes a separate listing
targeting a different buyer. Same effort to fulfill, many more lottery tickets in Etsy search.

Apply these styles across products:

- **Royal / Renaissance** (pet as a king/queen in oil-painting costume) — funny + giftable, huge seller
- **Minimalist line-art** (single continuous line from the photo) — modern home-decor buyers
- **Watercolor portrait** — soft, premium, memorial-friendly
- **Cartoon / "Bluey"-style flat illustration** — playful, young buyers *(draw your OWN style — never copy a real cartoon brand)*
- **Retro / 70s groovy text** ("Dog Mom" in retro font) — identity apparel trend
- **Photo-real collage** (the actual uploaded photos) — for blankets/ornaments

One "pet portrait mug" × 6 styles = 6 listings. That's the modular system, no code needed.

## 5. How personalization actually works (so you know the real "work")

1. Customer buys and uploads/sends their pet photo (Etsy's personalization box or a message).
2. You drop the photo into a **template** (Canva is fine to start) or apply the chosen style.
3. You upload the final art to the **Printify** product → Printify prints & ships to the buyer.
4. You keep the margin. Reviews roll in. Repeat.

Most "design" is **reusable templates** + photo placement — not drawing from scratch each
time. For illustrated styles (royal/line-art), you can use AI image tools or a cheap Fiverr
artist per order until volume justifies automating it. The custom **automation system** in
this repo takes over the order-routing/tracking/support once you're past the first sales.

## 6. Two rules to not get banned
- **Never use copyrighted/trademarked stuff** — no Disney, Bluey, sports teams, brand logos.
  Customer-uploaded *pet photos* are 100% fine; *your* original illustrations are fine.
- **Set clear processing times** (POD takes days) and **say "personalized item"** so buyers
  expect a proof/turnaround. Under-promise shipping; it protects your reviews.
